Safety and quality

Are CONNETIX magnetic tiles safe?

Yes. CONNETIX tiles are made from non-toxic ABS plastic that is free from BPA, phthalates and PFAS. Each tile is ultrasonically welded and secured with metal rivets, which reinforce the tile and keep the magnets securely enclosed.

CONNETIX PRO tiles are mechanically friction-fitted and secured with industrial-grade adhesive to keep magnets safely enclosed.

Are CONNETIX tiles safety tested?

CONNETIX states that its products undergo regular safety and quality testing and meet or exceed toy safety standards in the markets where they are sold. See Product safety and the official CONNETIX FAQs.

What age are CONNETIX for?

CONNETIX are recommended for children aged 3 and up. Younger children should always be supervised.

What are CONNETIX made from?

Non-toxic ABS plastic, with strong magnets securely enclosed inside each tile.

What makes CONNETIX different from other magnetic tiles?

Strong magnets, a signature bevelled edge, premium materials and regular safety testing, designed for open-ended play that grows with children.

Compatibility and care

Do CONNETIX work with other magnetic tile brands?

Yes, with most leading brands. Because CONNETIX magnets are stronger than most, connections with other brands may feel weaker.

Can I combine different CONNETIX packs?

Yes. Every CONNETIX pack is designed to work with every other, so a collection can grow over time.

How do I clean CONNETIX tiles?

Wipe them with a damp cloth and avoid harsh chemicals. Do not submerge tiles in water, as moisture can damage the magnets over time.

Are CONNETIX tiles waterproof?

No. Keep tiles out of water, including the bath and the pool.

Learning and development

Are CONNETIX educational?

Yes. Designed by educators, CONNETIX supports STEAM learning through hands-on exploration of design, engineering, geometry, magnetism and problem-solving. CONNETIX is MESH-accredited and used in classrooms around the world.

What skills do magnetic tiles help develop?

Fine motor skills, spatial awareness, creativity, problem-solving, critical thinking and collaboration. As children design, build and test their ideas, they also build persistence and confidence.

What is STEAM?

Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics, explored through hands-on, creative and inquiry-based play.

What is MESH?

MESH stands for Mental, Emotional and Social Health. The accreditation recognises products that support children's wellbeing, resilience and social development.
