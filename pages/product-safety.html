CONNETIX tiles are designed for children aged 3 and up. Every magnet is enclosed inside its tile with ultrasonic welding and metal rivets. CONNETIX PRO tiles are friction-fitted and secured with industrial-grade adhesive.

Using CONNETIX safely

  • Recommended for ages 3 and up. Supervise younger children.
  • Check tiles regularly. Stop using any tile that is cracked or damaged.
  • Keep tiles out of water and clean them with a damp cloth.
  • Light packs contain a rechargeable battery. Charge only with the supplied cable, under adult supervision.

Warning: magnets that are swallowed can stick together inside the body and cause serious injury. Seek medical help immediately if a magnet is swallowed.
