## Facts the client must supply

Delivery
- Delivery fees, and any free-delivery threshold
- Delivery times or estimates by emirate
- Courier or couriers used
- Order cut-off times, and whether same-day or next-day delivery is offered
- Which emirates and areas are covered, including remote areas and free zones
- Where UAE orders ship from (a UAE warehouse or overseas). If overseas, whether any customs duties or import charges apply.
- Whether tracking numbers will be added to every order, so Shopify's shipping confirmation includes tracking
- Cash on delivery availability
- Process for lost-in-transit parcels (CONNETIX's own "Lost-in-transit items" section is empty)

Returns and refunds
- UAE change-of-mind return window. For reference, the Australian policy uses "within 14 days of receiving your order". This must also go into the Shopify Refund policy at /policies/refund-policy.
- Who pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns (Australia: the customer)
- How returns are sent back (courier collection or customer drop-off) and the return address
- Refund method and timing (Australia: credit note or refund, "up to 10 business days")
- Items excluded from change-of-mind returns (Australia: discounted or sale items, and giveaway or competition products)
- Confirmation that the policy is consistent with UAE consumer protection law

Warranty
- Confirm the 30-day fault and damage period applies to UAE orders (the page carries a visible "[To confirm before launch]" note)
- Confirm CONNETIX covers all shipping costs for faulty, damaged or incorrect items in the UAE
- Confirm whether warranty claims are accepted for CONNETIX bought from other retailers in or outside the UAE
- Confirm the statement that the warranty applies to genuine CONNETIX products only
- Support response time for the UAE team (Australia: "aims to respond within three business days")

Contact and business details
- Customer support email address (Australia uses support@connetixtiles.com; confirm whether UAE customers should use it or a GCC address)
- Phone or WhatsApp number, if any
- Support hours and time zone
- Registered business name, trade licence and address for the UAE entity (CONNETIX GCC), if they should appear on any page or in the footer

Brand and retail
- Named awards, if the client wants them listed. connetixtiles.com only says "award-winning" and names none. A third-party article (communitynews.com.au) refers to a 2022 "Australian best STEAM toy" award; it could not be opened to verify, so nothing is named on the page.
- Founding year (not stated on connetixtiles.com)
- Any approved UAE retailers, and whether they will appear on the CONNETIX Store Locator
- Any official CONNETIX guidance on identifying counterfeit tiles or packaging features (none is published)
- Terms for UAE stockists (CONNETIX Australia publishes minimum order quantities and a B2B portal; not used because UAE terms are unknown)
- Whether the CONNETIX Education Program applies in the UAE

Safety and care
- Source and approval of the swallowed-magnet warning and light-pack charging guidance (taken from the existing product-safety draft)
- Any heat or direct-sun guidance for the UAE climate (for example, not leaving tiles in a hot car). CONNETIX publishes none, so none is given.
- UAE conformity certification (ECAS or G-Mark), already flagged on the product-safety draft
