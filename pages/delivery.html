We deliver CONNETIX orders across the UAE. The delivery options, fees and estimated delivery dates for your address are shown at checkout, before you pay.

Delivery options and fees

Enter your delivery address at checkout to see the delivery options available for your area, the fee for each option and the estimated delivery date. You'll see your order total, including delivery, before you confirm your order.

During sale periods, order processing and delivery may take a few extra days.

If you can't select your area at checkout, please contact us before placing your order.

Order confirmation and tracking

After you place your order, we'll email you an order confirmation with your order number. When your order ships, we'll email you again with tracking details so you can follow your delivery.

Please check your email address carefully at checkout. If our emails don't arrive, check your junk or spam folder.

Getting your address right

Clear address details help your parcel reach you first time. At checkout, please include:

  • Building or villa name and number, and your apartment or flat number
  • Street name, if there is one
  • Area or community, and emirate
  • A nearby landmark, such as a mall, school or mosque
  • A mobile number you can answer on the day of delivery, as the driver may need to call you for directions

If your building has reception or security, it can help to let them know a parcel is on its way.

Changing your delivery details

If you need to change your address or mobile number after ordering, contact us as soon as possible with your order number. We'll let you know whether the change can still be made.

If your parcel arrives damaged

If your parcel or any tiles arrive damaged, please contact us with your order number and a short description of the damage. We'll reply by email so you can send clear photos of the damaged item and its packaging.

Please keep the item and its packaging until we've been in touch, and don't let children play with any tile that is cracked or damaged. Damaged and faulty items are handled under our warranty. See Returns and warranty.

If your parcel hasn't arrived

Check your tracking details first. If tracking shows your order as delivered but you can't find it, check with your building reception, security or neighbours. If it still hasn't turned up, contact us with your order number.
