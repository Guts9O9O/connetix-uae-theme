CONNETIX was built on the belief that the best toys don't tell children what to create. They inspire children to imagine what could be possible.

Created by experts in learning and play

CONNETIX was founded in Perth, Australia, by Brea Brand and Dave Alexander, who shared a vision to create open-ended construction toys that inspire learning through play. With a Masters in early childhood education, Brea brought deep expertise in child development. Dave brought experience in mechanical design, manufacturing, logistics and entrepreneurship.

Today, that vision lives on through a passionate team of educators and experts dedicated to creating meaningful play experiences for children around the world.

Our mission

Our mission is to champion learning through play, with open-ended construction toys. The best construction toys don't tell children what to build. They invite them to think, test, adapt and imagine.

CONNETIX is designed by educators and parents and aligned with real learning principles. Our products are both STEAM and MESH accredited. MESH stands for Mental, Emotional and Social Health.

The values behind CONNETIX

As parents ourselves, we understand that the toys you welcome into your family's home need to be worthy of your trust. That's why we're committed to doing things with care, integrity and purpose, from the way our products are designed and tested to the way we run our business.

Made for years of creativity

At CONNETIX, every new idea starts with play. By watching how children explore, build and imagine, we create toys that grow with their curiosity. We don't innovate for innovation's sake. Every new product is designed to expand creative possibilities while maintaining the quality and durability families trust.

  • Strong magnets let children create taller and more complex structures.
  • On standard tiles, ultrasonic welding and metal rivets keep magnets securely enclosed and add durability.
  • Our signature bevelled design enhances durability while creating beautiful light refractions.
  • Tiles are made from non-toxic ABS plastic that is free from BPA, phthalates and PFAS.
  • CONNETIX products undergo regular safety and quality testing and are recommended for ages 3 and up.
