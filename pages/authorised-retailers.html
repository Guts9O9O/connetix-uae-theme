Why buying genuine matters

Many magnetic tiles look alike, but what's inside each tile matters. Genuine CONNETIX tiles are:

  • Made from non-toxic ABS plastic that is free from BPA, lead, phthalates and PFAS
  • Built to keep their magnets securely enclosed. Standard tiles are ultrasonically welded and secured with metal rivets. CONNETIX PRO tiles are friction-fitted and secured with industrial-grade adhesive.
  • Regularly tested to meet or exceed toy safety standards in every market where CONNETIX is sold
  • Safety tested for ages 3 and up

Magnet safety is the reason this matters most. Magnets that are swallowed can stick together inside the body and cause serious injury. We can only stand behind the materials, construction and testing of tiles made by CONNETIX. See Product safety for the standards CONNETIX is tested against.

How to recognise genuine CONNETIX

  • Check the name. The brand is spelled CONNETIX. "Kinetics" is a common misspelling, and CONNETIX is the original brand name.
  • Know the details. CONNETIX tiles have strong magnets, metal rivets on standard tiles and our signature bevelled design.
  • Check the seller. Shop here, or with a stockist approved by CONNETIX. Approved stockists are featured on the CONNETIX Store Locator.
