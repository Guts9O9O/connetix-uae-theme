We hope you love every CONNETIX product you receive. If something isn't right, here's how we can help. Please contact us before sending anything back, so we can guide you through the right next step.

Changed your mind?

We understand that sometimes a purchase isn't quite right. You can request a return within the return period stated in our Refund policy, provided:

  • You contact us first with your order number and the items you'd like to return.
  • Items are unused and in their original, undamaged packaging.

Once we've confirmed your return, we'll let you know how to send the items back. Please pack them securely so they arrive safely. Refunds are processed once your return has been received and inspected. See our Refund policy for full details, including how refunds are made and any items that can't be returned.

We may decline returns that fall outside the return period, or items that have been used, damaged or returned without their original product packaging.

Faulty or damaged items

We take great pride in the quality of every CONNETIX product, and every tile undergoes careful quality checks. Manufacturing issues are rare, but if something doesn't go to plan, we're committed to working with you to find the best solution.

What our warranty covers

A product may be considered faulty if it arrives damaged or develops a manufacturing fault within 30 days of receiving your order.

Where a product is deemed faulty, damaged or no longer safe for play, CONNETIX will do its best to provide a like-for-like replacement. If a replacement, exchange or return is needed because CONNETIX supplied an incorrect, faulty or damaged item, CONNETIX will cover all associated shipping costs.

What our warranty doesn't cover

Our warranty does not cover damage caused by:

  • Incorrect assembly
  • Inadequate storage
  • The use of inappropriate cleaning products
  • Misuse, abuse or neglect
  • Not following our recommended care and maintenance guidelines. See Caring for your tiles.

Due to the manufacturing process, minor cosmetic marks can occasionally occur. Where these slight imperfections don't affect how the product works or plays, they may not be eligible for replacement.

How to make a claim

If you believe you've received a faulty or damaged item, stop using it and contact us with:

  • Your full name and email address
  • Your location
  • Your CONNETIX order number, or the name of the retailer where you made your purchase
  • A short description of the issue

We'll reply by email and ask for clear photos and/or videos showing the issue. In some cases, we may request additional photos or videos before deciding on the most appropriate resolution. Every claim is assessed on a case-by-case basis.

Received the wrong item?

Please contact us with your order number. We'll send the correct product once we've received the incorrect item, or once you've confirmed that you've sent it back. The returned item must be unused and in its original packaging. We'll send the correct product to your original delivery address unless we agree otherwise in writing, and we'll reimburse your return shipping using the method we determine is most suitable.

Bought from another retailer?

If you bought CONNETIX from another authorised retailer and would like a return or exchange, please contact that retailer directly.
