CONNETIX tiles are built to withstand years of play. A little care keeps them clean, safe and ready for the next build.

Cleaning your tiles

  • Wipe tiles with a damp cloth.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals and strong cleaning products.
  • Don't submerge tiles in water, as moisture may damage the magnets over time. That means no soaking in the sink or bath, and no dishwasher or washing machine.
  • Let tiles dry fully before you put them away.

CONNETIX tiles are not waterproof, so keep them out of the bath, the pool and water play.

Storing your tiles

Keep your collection in a clean, dry place. Some builders want every shape sorted by colour, while others prefer to tip everything into one basket. Ideas that work well:

  • A storage basket to start simple
  • Tote trays to organise tiles by colour
  • Drawer units for growing collections
  • Rolling storage crates or a metal trolley to keep tiles easy to reach

Checking for damage

Check your tiles regularly, especially after rough play or a fall. Look for:

  • Cracks, chips or splits in the plastic
  • Seams that have come apart
  • A magnet that is exposed or coming loose

If you find a damaged or faulty tile, stop using it and contact us within 14 calendar days of delivery with your order number and photos. See Warranty information.

Warning: magnets that are swallowed can stick together inside the body and cause serious injury. Seek medical help immediately if a magnet is swallowed.
