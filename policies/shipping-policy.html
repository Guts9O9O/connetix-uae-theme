Delivery destinations

We deliver across the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), including the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman. Delivery availability is subject to confirmation for the address entered at checkout. Orders are dispatched from our UAE warehouse.

Delivery charges

We offer free delivery across the GCC. Any applicable delivery terms will be shown at checkout before you complete your order.

Estimated delivery times

Estimated delivery is usually:

  • United Arab Emirates: 1–3 days.
  • Other GCC destinations: 5–7 days.

These timeframes are estimates and are not guaranteed delivery dates. Actual delivery may vary depending on the destination, order processing, customs procedures where applicable, public holidays, remote-area access and circumstances outside our reasonable control. Where available, the estimated delivery timeframe will be shown at checkout or in your order confirmation.

Order information

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address and an active contact number when placing your order. If you need to request an address change, contact us as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee changes after an order has been dispatched.

Courier and tracking

We arrange delivery through our logistics partners. Delivery or tracking information will be shared where available. We do not identify a specific courier in this policy.

Payment on delivery

Cash on delivery is not available. Orders must be paid using an available payment method displayed at checkout.
