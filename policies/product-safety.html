Safety and care information for CONNETIX products, based on the official CONNETIX global website. Always follow the age grading, safety warnings and care instructions supplied with your product. If the packaging or product instructions give more specific guidance, that guidance takes precedence.

Recommended age

CONNETIX products are recommended for children aged 3 years and up. Younger children should always be supervised.

Materials and construction

Standard magnetic tiles: CONNETIX states that standard tiles are made from non-toxic ABS plastic and are free from BPA, phthalates and PFAS. Magnets are ultrasonically welded and secured with metal rivets.

CONNETIX PRO: CONNETIX describes PRO tiles as mechanically friction-fitted and secured with industrial-grade adhesive.

Safety and quality testing

CONNETIX states that its products undergo regular safety and quality testing and meet or exceed toy safety standards in the markets where they are sold. For CONNETIX's own safety and testing information, see the official CONNETIX FAQs and About our products.

Care

Wipe your tiles with a damp cloth and avoid harsh chemicals. Do not submerge tiles or place them in water, as moisture may damage the magnets. More tips are on Caring for your tiles.

Product-specific information

Each product page shows the recommended age and the details for that pack. Light packs and other special products may include their own instructions in the box. Please read and keep them.
