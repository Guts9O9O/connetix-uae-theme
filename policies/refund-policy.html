Our commitment

Change of mind and personal preference

We do not accept returns or refunds solely because a customer changes their mind or does not like the product’s colour, design, appearance, size or style after delivery, where the correct product has been supplied and it matches the description and specifications presented at the time of purchase. This policy does not exclude, restrict or waive any consumer rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable UAE law.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect products – 14-day notification

If your order arrives damaged, has a manufacturing fault, or contains an incorrect product, please contact us within 14 calendar days from the date you receive the order. Please provide:

  • Your order number and the name/email used to place the order.

  • A brief description of the issue.

  • Clear photographs or videos showing the product and the reported issue.

Please contact our support team before returning an item. We will review your request and provide instructions.

Assessment and inspection

All reported issues are subject to assessment. We may request additional information, photographs or videos, and may arrange for the product to be returned for physical inspection. The appropriate resolution will be determined based on the inspection findings, the nature of the issue, the applicable manufacturer warranty and mandatory consumer rights under UAE law.

Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, improper storage or handling, unauthorised modification, or failure to follow the manufacturer's care instructions may not qualify as a manufacturing fault, subject to applicable law and the relevant warranty terms.

Resolutions and refunds
