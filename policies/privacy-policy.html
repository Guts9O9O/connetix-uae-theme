This Privacy Policy explains how TRUCARE FZC, trading as CONNETIX UAE (we, us or our), collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit or use our website, create an account, place an order, contact our team or otherwise interact with our online store.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use our store, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, billing and delivery address, order details, account information, payment status and transaction references, customer-service communications, device and browser information, IP address, and information about how you interact with our website. Payment card details are handled by the relevant payment service provider; we do not intend to store full payment card details on our own systems.

How we use information

We use personal information to operate our online store, process and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, facilitate payments, provide customer support, manage returns and warranty requests, maintain customer accounts, prevent fraud, improve our website and services, comply with legal obligations, and send marketing communications where permitted or where you have opted in.

Shopify, payments and service providers

Our online store is hosted on Shopify. Personal information may be processed by Shopify and service providers that support our operations, including payment processors, Tabby (where selected and available), delivery and logistics partners, technology providers, analytics providers and customer-support tools. These providers process information under their applicable terms, privacy notices and contractual arrangements.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the store, remember preferences, support security, understand website usage and improve the shopping experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

When we share information

We may share relevant information with service providers, payment providers, delivery partners, professional advisers, regulators or public authorities where necessary to provide our services, comply with law, protect our rights, or respond to valid legal requests. We do not sell customer personal information.

International processing

Some service providers may process or store personal information outside the UAE. Where this occurs, we will take steps required by applicable data protection laws and our service-provider arrangements.

Retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order administration, customer support, accounting, legal compliance and dispute resolution. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to update your marketing preferences. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where legally required or permitted. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the message. We may still send essential service communications about orders, accounts or support requests.

Children

Our online store is intended for adults making purchases. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. A parent or legal guardian may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@trucarefzc.com or +971 50 410 3201.
